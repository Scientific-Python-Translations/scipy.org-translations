Reddy, David Cournapeau, Evgeni Burovski, Pearu Peterson, Warren
Weckesser, Jonathan Bright, Stéfan J. van der Walt, Matthew Brett,
Joshua Wilson, K. Jarrod Millman, Nikolay Mayorov, Andrew R. J. Nelson,
